---
title: Glossary
description: Plain-English definitions of the core AI terms, each with the mistake people usually make.
---

Each term gets one sentence. The line under it is the common mix-up.

- **Model.** The AI engine that turns your instructions and context into an answer.
  *It is only one ingredient in the result.*
- **Prompt.** The request you are making right now.
  *A good one states the outcome you want, not just the topic.*
- **Context.** Everything the AI can see while it works on your request.
  *The right material helps. More material does not.*
- **Token.** A small piece of text. Models read and write in tokens, and billing is counted in them.
  *You will not count them. Just know that bigger jobs cost more.*
- **Tool.** A capability the AI can call to look something up or take an action, such as search or a database query.
  *Tools give it power, so they need permission limits and checking.*
- **Connector.** A link between the AI and a real system such as Jira, Slack, or Drive.
  *Being connected does not mean it should touch everything inside.*
- **MCP.** An open standard for plugging tools and connectors into AI.
  *It is plumbing. It does not make the AI smarter or safer.*
- **Skill (or recipe).** A saved procedure the AI can run again for a job you repeat.
  *Products call it a skill, command, or template. It is the same idea.*
- **Agent.** AI that picks its own next steps toward a goal instead of giving one answer.
  *More freedom is not automatically better than a plain chat.*
- **Workflow.** A fixed path of steps, checks, and handoffs that runs the same way every time.
  *Repeatable value comes from this, not from one lucky answer.*
- **Eval.** A repeatable test of whether an AI setup does a job well enough.
  *Five of your own real examples beat a public leaderboard.*
- **Benchmark.** A public test used to compare models.
  *Treat a score as marketing until you know how it was produced.*
- **Spec (or durable brief).** A written record of what you want, why, and what was decided.
  *It lets the work outlive the chat and the person who started it.*
- **Hallucination.** When AI states something false with full confidence.
  *It is not lying. It is guessing. That is why you check.*
